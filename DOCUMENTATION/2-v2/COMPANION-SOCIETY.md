# The companion society (ideas analysed and decided 2026-10-22)

The user's companions should feel like a small society: relationships with each other, friendships that grow, group activities, drama, and a social life you can watch. Source: an external idea list ([CHATGPT-COMPANION-IDEAS-1](../6-v1-reference/EXTERNAL/CHATGPT-COMPANION-IDEAS-1.md)), analysed against what the app has and decided with the user on 2026-10-22. Related: [COMPANION-PLAN](COMPANION-PLAN.md) (what's built), [COMPANION-SOCIAL-IDEAS](COMPANION-SOCIAL-IDEAS.md) (help pages, info page, invites, gift tastes), [JOURNEY-DESIGN](JOURNEY-DESIGN.md) (the hub). Build order: below, and in [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md).

Everything runs on the phone with rules, weights and pre-written lines: no AI, no servers.

## What the user decided

| Question | Decided |
|---|---|
| Where does their social life happen? | **Mostly in the Journey hub** (its rooms are the places), **and often on the normal app screens too**: companions meet, chat and play on screen regularly. |
| Friends across fandoms? | **Yes, slowly by meeting.** Within a fandom they start with their canon relationships; across fandoms they start as strangers and become friends or rivals by meeting in the app. |
| Romance between companions? | **Only between two adults or two minors, no exceptions.** Adults can be couples and still include you (polyamory: you can have several Lovers, a couple can both be yours). Minors' relationships stay innocent (sweethearts: holding hands, blushing, valentines; nothing more, never lover lines). |
| Minors and you | **Minors never get Lover status or lover lines.** Their closest levels with you are family and bestie: they treat you like a parent or big sibling and sometimes slip ("Thanks, dad! ...I mean, {name}!"). The word they use is a **setting in your profile** (dad, mom, parent, big brother, big sister, big sib). |
| Who is a minor? | By canon age: **all South Park kids** (Cartman, Kyle, Stan, Kenny, Wendy, Bebe, Butters), **Morty and Summer**, **Luke**, **Spike**. Everyone else is an adult. |
| Drama | **Both:** mostly light and playful, and **now and then real drama** (an argument that lasts a while and is resolved later), true to the characters' dynamics. Never about you or your absence, never something you have to fix. |
| Personality categories | **Every companion gets one category** (9, picked from the current characters, below). Some categories get along easily, some clash; each companion also **dislikes one category**. |

## Personality categories

Picked to fit the 35 current characters; 9 instead of 8 because the hot-headed, stubborn ones (Applejack, Kyle, Mammon) fit nowhere else. The 7 traits (playful, proud, …) stay as they are; the category is one extra field.

| Category | Who they are | Characters |
|---|---|---|
| **Leader** | In charge, dignified, responsible | Lucifer, Princess Luna, Diavolo, Wendy, Beth |
| **Brainy** | Curious, bookish, clever | Twilight Sparkle, Satan, Solomon |
| **Energetic** | Loud, cheerful, sporty, eager | Pinkie Pie, Rainbow Dash, Luke, Spike, Jerry |
| **Gentle** | Shy, soft, anxious, sweet | Fluttershy, Leviathan, Butters, Morty |
| **Glamorous** | Stylish, dramatic, flirty | Rarity, Asmodeus, Bebe, Summer |
| **Chaotic** | Mischief, schemes, pranks | Discord, Rick, Cartman, Randy |
| **Laid-back** | Chill, sleepy, food-loving | Belphegor, Beelzebub, Stan, Kenny |
| **Caring** | Warm, parental, wise | Princess Celestia, Mi Amore Cadenza, Simeon |
| **Fiery** | Honest, stubborn, quick-tempered | Applejack, Kyle, Mammon |

How categories get along (a starting point; canon relationships always win):

| Category | Gets along easily with | Clashes with |
|---|---|---|
| Leader | Brainy, Caring | Chaotic, Laid-back |
| Brainy | Leader, Gentle | Energetic |
| Energetic | Energetic, Glamorous, Fiery | Laid-back, Brainy |
| Gentle | Gentle, Caring, Brainy | Chaotic, Fiery |
| Glamorous | Glamorous, Energetic | Fiery, Laid-back |
| Chaotic | Chaotic, Energetic | Leader, Fiery |
| Laid-back | Laid-back, Caring, Gentle | Leader, Energetic |
| Caring | Gentle, Laid-back, Leader | (mild) Chaotic |
| Fiery | Energetic, Caring | Chaotic, Glamorous |

Each character also gets one personal **dislike** (a category), chosen from canon when there is one (Lucifer dislikes Laid-back because of Belphegor, Applejack dislikes Glamorous because of Rarity's fussing, Kyle dislikes Chaotic because of Cartman).

## The ideas, one by one

| # | Idea | Possible? | How it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Relationships between companions** (best friends, friends, rivals, siblings, crushes, partners, mentor/student, fans) | Yes | A table of pairs with a type and a score. Canon relationships come with the character files (the Obey Me brothers are siblings, Kyle and Cartman rivals, Beth and Jerry married); everyone else starts as strangers. Also what per-pair conversations (roadmap 3.1) need. |
| 2 | **Group activities** (tea party, picnic, board games, dance, movie night, group nap, storytelling, music, exercise, hide-and-seek, talent show, gossip, crafting) | Yes, in two sizes | Small ones (2 or 3 companions) on screen; bigger gatherings in the hub's rooms. Shown with movement and emoji props (🧺, 🎲, 🎬, 💤), since companions are round pictures. Participation fits the category (Gentle watches, Energetic starts the dance, Laid-back falls asleep during the movie, Chaotic cheats at games). |
| 3 | **Kinds of visits** (casual, to see a friend, group, surprise, invitation, sleepover, following a friend, goodbye) and **arrival and departure rituals** | Yes | Builds on the existing visits. Rituals with transform/opacity only: walking in from the side, dropping from the top, peeking over the edge, arriving together, running to a favourite, waving, dozing off and fading, leaving together. |
| 4 | **Social personality values** (sociability, energy, independence, mischief, shyness…) | Yes, mostly already there | Derived from the 7 traits, the movement setting and the category instead of ten new numbers. They change chances, never decide outright. |
| 5 | **Friendships that grow** between companions (stranger → acquaintance → friend → close → best friend), **shared memories**, **social milestones** | Yes | Grows by itself when they meet; you never have to manage it. Memories are event records with pre-written lines ("Remember our picnic?"). Milestones unlock new interactions. |
| 6 | **Daily rhythm and events** (morning greetings, afternoon games, evening gatherings, night naps; weekly talent show; seasons) | Yes | Time of day changes which activities are likely. Weekly and seasonal events reuse the activity system; calendar moments exist already. |
| 7 | **Games between companions** (rock-paper-scissors, races, cards, trivia, arm wrestling; cooperative tower or puzzle) with consequences | Yes | Short scenes that play themselves with a weighted result; winners cheer, Fiery ones demand a rematch, Caring ones console. No betting. |
| 8 | **Gifts and belongings between companions** (also borrowing and returning) | Yes | Small items per character (books from Brainy ones, odd things from Chaotic ones); recorded in the feed. |
| 9 | **Locations** (living room, garden, café, library, park, music room, game room, workshop…) | Yes, in the hub | The hub's rooms (today's Guild Hall rooms grow into these). Two friends can "leave for the café" and come back later. |
| 10 | **Social reactions to you** (two wave together, one introduces a visitor, shows off a gift, asks you to choose an activity) | Yes, carefully | Only occasional and only good news; "what happened while you were away" is told as their fun, never as something you missed. |
| 11 | **A social feed** (activity log, friendship overview, recent visitors, memories, collection of interactions seen) | Yes | A page (and a corner of the hub). What happened while the app was closed is worked out when you open it, a few items at most. |
| 12 | **Mischief and disagreements** with resolutions (apology, handshake, a mediator, a game, a gift) | Yes, decided: light plus occasional real drama | Real drama runs over a day or two and always resolves; never involves you, never needs you. Chosen by relationship, category clashes and canon dynamics. |
| 13 | **Hidden and rare interactions**, a discovery collection | Yes | Unique pair or trio interactions, birthday specials, rivals cooperating; the collection shows which you've seen. |
| 14 | **Socially aware visitor picking** | Yes, easy | Friends of whoever is on screen come more often; recently seen ones less; ones with something new to show a bit more. Still random enough to surprise. |
| 15 | **Roles in group activities** (organiser, enthusiast, reluctant one, troublemaker) | Yes | Picked when an activity starts, from categories; one activity template gives many variations. |
| 16 | **Architecture**: data-driven companions, reusable interaction templates, a state machine, small saved data, three content layers | Yes, as proposed | 35 companions make 595 pairs, so lines come in three layers: general (any pair), by relationship type, and unique for favourite pairs. |

What changes from the source: no limbs or body animations (movement and emoji props instead), drama never about you, the feed never phrased as "you missed this", and the age rules above.
