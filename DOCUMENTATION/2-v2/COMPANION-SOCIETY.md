# The companion society (ideas analysed and decided 2026-10-22)

The user's companions should feel like a small society: relationships with each other, friendships that grow, group activities, drama, and a social life you can watch. Source: an external idea list ([CHATGPT-COMPANION-IDEAS-1](../6-v1-reference/EXTERNAL/CHATGPT-COMPANION-IDEAS-1.md)), analysed against what the app has and decided with the user on 2026-10-22. Related: [COMPANION-PLAN](COMPANION-PLAN.md) (what's built), [COMPANION-SOCIAL-IDEAS](COMPANION-SOCIAL-IDEAS.md) (help pages, info page, invites, gift tastes), [JOURNEY-DESIGN](JOURNEY-DESIGN.md) (the hub). Build order: below, and in [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md).

Everything runs on the phone with rules, weights and pre-written lines: no AI, no servers.

## What the user decided

| Question | Decided |
|---|---|
| Where does their social life happen? | **Mostly in the Journey hub** (its rooms are the places), **and often on the normal app screens too**: companions meet, chat and play on screen regularly. **In the hub up to 7 can hang out together** (added 2026-10-22), so existing friend groups are found together when they're friends: the Mane Six (with Spike), the seven Obey Me brothers, the South Park boys. On the normal screens it stays at up to 3. |
| Friends across fandoms? | **Yes, slowly by meeting.** Within a fandom they start with their canon relationships; across fandoms they start as strangers and become friends or rivals by meeting in the app. |
| Romance between companions? | **Only between two adults or two minors, no exceptions.** Adults can be couples and still include you (polyamory: you can have several Lovers, a couple can both be yours). Minors' relationships stay innocent (sweethearts: holding hands, blushing, valentines; nothing more, never lover lines). |
| Minors and you | **Minors never get Lover status or lover lines.** Their closest levels with you are family and bestie: they treat you like a parent or big sibling and sometimes slip ("Thanks, dad! ...I mean, {name}!"). The word they use is a **setting in your profile** (dad, mom, parent, big brother, big sister, big sib). |
| Who is a minor? | By canon age: **all South Park kids** (Cartman, Kyle, Stan, Kenny, Wendy, Bebe, Butters), **Morty and Summer**, **Luke**, **Spike**. Everyone else is an adult. |
| Drama | **Both:** mostly light and playful, and **now and then real drama** (an argument that lasts a while), true to the characters' dynamics. **Drama doesn't always resolve** (added 2026-10-22): companions can fall out for good and become enemies; enemies avoid or snipe at each other, and may (rarely) make up much later. Never about your absence, never something you have to fix. **Drama over you is allowed, in a flattering way** (added 2026-10-22, see below). |
| Personality categories | **Every companion gets one category** (9, picked from the current characters, below). Some categories get along easily, some clash; each companion also **dislikes one category**. |

## Drama over you (added 2026-10-22)

Two kinds, both cute and flattering, never a problem you have to solve:

- **Jealous over you:** now and then, when two companions who are both close to you meet, they bicker over you ("{name} likes MY cooking better!", "I was here first!"). Between two adults it can be romantic-jealous, especially when either is your Lover ("Back off, they're mine." "Says who?"). As soon as a minor is involved it's only "who's your favourite" rivalry, like siblings or best friends competing for you, never romantic.
- **Protective of you:** when someone close to you meets a companion you're on bad terms with (very low closeness, your "enemy"), they take your side and bicker with them ("You were mean to {name}? Oh, we're going to have words.").

How it plays out: a short exchange of lines on screen (or a scene in the hub), sometimes ending with you being asked to pick a side as a joke (any answer is fine and it costs nothing), sometimes with them making up. It happens now and then, not every time they meet, and never on a hard day (mood 1 or 2): then they're only kind. Needs new line moments per character (jealous bickering, jealous reply, protective, making up), in the files like the others.

## Plans with you: invitations, a calendar and a feed (added 2026-10-22)

Companions invite you to do things, and the society keeps its own calendar and feed, so you can roleplay a social life alongside real life:

- **One companion invites you:** "Want to get an iced coffee with me tomorrow around noon?", "Play games tonight?", "Walk with me after dinner?". Between adults (and you) it can be a date; minors only invite you to hangouts (games, baking, homework together).
- **A friend group invites you** (the Mane Six, the brothers, or any group where everyone is close with each other and with you): the movies, a picnic, a game night, a beach day.
- **They plan things among themselves** too ("Pinkie is throwing a party on Saturday"), which shows up in their calendar and feed whether or not you're invited.
- **A calendar** of upcoming plans (yours with them, and theirs), and **a feed** like a little social network: posts, photos (emoji scenes), "who went where".
- **Roleplay with real life:** you say yes, and when the time comes you do the activity yourself in real life (game on your own, make an iced coffee, watch a film). The app plays a small cutscene of the two of you (or the group) doing it, and it lands in the feed and in the companion's memories ("Remember our game night?"). Real-life links count where they fit (a walk logs as a walk).
- **Rulebook:** saying no or "maybe later" is always fine and in character; a plan you miss is never a problem ("We'll go another time!"), nothing is counted against you, and plans pause on a hard day except for gentle ones (a quiet tea together). Reminders for a plan are optional and gentle, like the other notifications.

## Homes (added 2026-10-22)

Every companion lives somewhere, and you can visit them at home, once their home is free:

- **Their own homes, from canon by default.** Some share: the seven Obey Me brothers live together in the House of Lamentation; Diavolo in the Demon Lord's Castle; Simeon, Luke and Solomon in Purgatory Hall; Celestia and Luna in Canterlot Castle; Twilight and Spike in her castle; the Smiths (Rick, Morty, Summer, Beth, Jerry) in one house; Stan and Randy in the Marsh house. Others live alone: Fluttershy's cottage, Rarity's boutique, Applejack's farm, Pinkie at Sugarcube Corner, Rainbow Dash's cloud house, Cadance in the Crystal Empire, Discord in his own chaos realm; each South Park kid at their family's house.
- **Adults can move in together** when they get along well (a high relationship score), and move apart again if they fall out. Minors always live with their canon family; they never move in with someone else.
- **Visiting a home needs it to be freed:** its region must be discovered on the Journey map and that region's big boss defeated. Until then the home is shown as "not reachable yet" (never as something you failed at).
- **Travelers have no home to visit.** A companion whose world isn't in the game yet (the existing "traveler" home region) only turns up in places you already know: visiting another companion, or at a public inn or café. When its world is added later, it gets a home like the others.
- **Your own home:** you have a home too (the Journey hub), which you upgrade slowly (rooms, furniture, decorations, paid with what real life earns). You can invite companions over for activities (game night, movie night, cooking, a picnic in your garden) and **sleepovers**. Sleepovers are for adults only; minors come to daytime and evening activities.
- Visiting someone's home or having them over uses the same visit, plan and activity system as the rest of the society (invitations, cutscenes, the feed, memories).
