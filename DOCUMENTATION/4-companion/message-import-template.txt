HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE
========================

This file is a template for the "Import All Categories" button, which
appears in two places:
  - Companions page (Me > Companions > New Companion / edit a companion)
  - Personality Groups page (Me > Companions > Personality Groups)

Personality Groups are a shared message pool multiple companions can draw
from alongside their own custom lines — editing a group's messages updates
every companion using it immediately. A companion's final message pool for
any category is its own lines PLUS its group's lines (if it belongs to one).

12 personality groups come built in (Me > Companions > Personality Groups),
seeded automatically the first time the app opens: The Scholar, The
Daredevil, The Gentle Soul, The Diva, The Party Starter, The Steadfast, and
The Regal Guide (personality archetypes, not likenesses, inspired by the
classic "one of each type" friend-group dynamic), plus five originals —
The Coach, The Zen Guide, The Sidekick, The Cheerleader, and The Sage.
Delete any you don't want; they won't be re-seeded. See
src/lib/defaultPersonalityGroups.ts for the full source of truth.

Rules the importer understands:

1. A header line starts a new category. Any of these formats work:
     [Achievement Unlocked]
     Achievement Unlocked:
     # Achievement Unlocked
   Matching is case-insensitive and ignores spaces/punctuation, so
   "achievement_unlocked", "Achievement Unlocked", and
   "[ACHIEVEMENT-UNLOCKED]" all match the same category.

2. Every line under a header becomes one message in that category,
   one message per line. Blank lines are ignored.

3. Any lines BEFORE the first header go into "General".

4. Wrapping quotes are stripped automatically, so it's fine to write
   lines either as  Hello there  or  "Hello there".

5. Use {name} anywhere you want the user's own name inserted.

6. [Lover] is a special extra category — only available when importing
   into a companion (not a Personality Group), and only takes effect if
   that companion is marked as a Lover. It fills the "Lover Messages" pool,
   not a normal event category.

7. Importing ADDS to whatever messages are already there, it does not
   replace them. Safe to re-run with more lines later.

WHAT EACH CATEGORY IS ACTUALLY USED FOR
========================================

General / Default
  Said when you tap the companion bubble directly. Also the fallback
  used for any category below that has no messages of its own.

Morning Greeting
  On app open, between 03:00–11:30, with a high (~70%) chance.

Welcome Back
  On app open outside the morning window, and every time you open
  the Journey.

Achievement Unlocked
  When you unlock an achievement (checked when visiting the
  Achievements page), or when you successfully claim a finished quest
  or defeat a boss in the Journey.

Habit Completed
  When you check off a habit on the To-Do page.

Task Completed
  When you check off a task on the To-Do page. Separate pool from
  Habit Completed so you can give tasks and habits different tones.

Low Mood Support
  During idle moments, if your most recently logged mood score was 3
  or below — high (~70%) chance of showing instead of a plain idle
  message.

Fasting Goal Met
  When you break a fast that reached its goal length.

Streak Milestone
  Not wired to an automatic trigger yet — reserved for a future streak
  milestone feature. Messages here currently only show if something
  else calls this category directly.

Phone-Free Reminder
  Repeats continuously (not just once) during your phone-free window:
  from 1 hour before your set sleep time through 1 hour after your
  wake time.

Points Earned
  Any time you earn points from any logged action.

Weight Loss
  When a new (non-backfilled) weight entry is lower than your
  previous one.

Weight Gain / Plateau
  When a new (non-backfilled) weight entry is higher than your
  previous one. Kept gentle, not guilt-driven.

Exercise Logged
  Any time you log an exercise session.

Water Goal Met
  The moment your water total for the day crosses the 2L goal.

Sleep Logged
  Any time you log last night's sleep.

Personal Best
  When you set a new all-time record — currently tracked for longest
  fast and most weight lost to date. Meant to feel rare and special.

Level Up
  Checked once per app open — fires if your Character level went up,
  or your character class changed, since the last time you opened
  the app. See Me > Character.

Boss Defeated
  When you claim victory over a Journey boss.

Goodnight
  A one-time wind-down nudge in the 30 minutes right before your
  Phone-Free window starts (90–60 minutes before your set sleep time).

First-Time Milestone
  The very first time you ever log a meal, exercise session, weight
  entry, habit completion, task completion, or finish a book.

Idle / AFK
  Fallback shown after ~2 minutes of no interaction, when none of
  the higher-priority conditions above (phone-free, goodnight, mood,
  affinity) apply. Sometimes replaced by a data-aware nudge (e.g.
  overdue tasks, low water) or, for a Lover companion with high
  affinity, one of their Lover Messages instead.

Daily Check Reminder
  Phone notification (Android app) at the time set in Settings ->
  Notifications, only while today's check is still open. The
  companion's name is the notification title.

Floor Reminder
  Phone notification (Android app) in the evening, only while your
  daily floor isn't done yet. Keep it gentle: even one counts.
